🎯 Why Posture Matters
Good posture makes muscle look bigger and waist look smaller
A forward head and rounded shoulders hide back definition
Anterior pelvic tilt can flatten glutes visually
10 mins daily posture work = visible difference within weeks
🧠 Daily Posture Habits
Ears over shoulders over hips — check in a mirror daily
Chin tucked slightly — not forward
Shoulders back and down — not shrugged
Ribcage stacked over pelvis — not flared
Slight posterior tilt when standing — glutes lightly on
Feet hip-width, weight even — not rocking to one side
At desk: screen at eye level, elbows at 90 degrees
Phone: raise it to eye level — stop looking down

Anterior pelvic tilt — glute activation, hip flexor stretching
Rib flare — 90/90 breathing already in your plan helps this
Hyperextended knees — soft knee habit when standing

📋 About This Program
6 weeks of daily practice to unlock hamstrings and achieve a full forward fold
Consistency is everything — 15 min daily beats 2 hours once a week
Never bounce or force a stretch — pain means stop immediately
Morning is great for activation — evening is better for stretching
Tight hip flexors will fight you — add a 60s kneeling lunge stretch daily
Film yourself monthly so you can actually see your progress
Week 1 — Activate & Awaken (15 min daily)
Learn the posterior pelvic tilt, wake up glutes and abs
Supine posterior pelvic tilt — 3 sets / 10 x 5s holds
↳ Flatten your lower back into the floor. Breathe normally throughout.
Dead bug (bent knee) — 2 sets / 8 each side
↳ Press lower back flat before lowering each leg. Slow and controlled.
Glute bridge — 3 sets / 10 reps
↳ Tuck tailbone at the top. Squeeze glutes fully.
Seated forward fold (passive) — 3 sets / 30s hold
↳ Legs straight, reach forward without straining. Just breathe into it.
Week 2 — Lengthen the Back Chain (20 min daily)
Actively stretch hamstrings with pelvic control
Standing posterior pelvic tilt — 3 sets / 10 x 5s holds
↳ Tuck tailbone under while standing. Abs gently engaged.
Supine hamstring stretch (strap/towel) — 3 sets / 45s each leg
↳ Keep lower back flat. Pull leg toward you gently, no bouncing.
Glute bridge with tilt — 3 sets / 12 reps
↳ Posterior tilt at the top of every single rep.
Standing forward fold (soft knees) — 3 sets / 45s
↳ Bend knees slightly, hinge from hips, let your head hang heavy.
Week 3 — Hip Hinge Mastery (20 min daily)
Learn to fold from hips, not the lower back
Wall hip hinge drill — 3 sets / 10 reps
↳ Stand 6in from wall. Push hips back to touch it. Keep back flat.
Cat-cow with pelvic focus — 2 sets / 10 slow reps
↳ Exaggerate the posterior tilt on the cat phase.
Single-leg hamstring stretch — 3 sets / 45s each
↳ One leg extended, other bent. Hinge forward with a flat back.
Standing forward fold (straighter legs) — 3 sets / 60s
↳ Gradually straighten legs week by week. Never force it.
Week 4 — Depth & Control (25 min daily)
Increase fold depth while maintaining pelvic position
Active hamstring flexion (lying) — 3 sets / 12 each side
↳ Lift straight leg, hold 2s at top. Keep pelvis neutral throughout.
Pyramid pose hold — 3 sets / 60s each side
↳ Front leg straight. Hinge chest toward shin. Breathe into tightness.
Seated straddle fold — 3 sets / 60s
↳ Legs wide. Walk hands forward from hips. Keep spine long.
Standing forward fold — 3 sets / 60-90s
↳ Feet hip-width. Grab elbows, sway gently. Surrender into the stretch.
Week 5 — Progressive Loading (25 min daily)
Add gentle load and longer holds to deepen flexibility
Weighted forward fold (light) — 3 sets / 60s
↳ Hold a 2-5lb weight or book. Let gravity assist the fold.
Posterior tilt + march (standing) — 3 sets / 10 each leg
↳ Maintain pelvic tilt while slowly lifting knees alternately.
Down dog to forward fold flow — 3 sets / 5 slow reps
↳ Walk feet to hands, hold fold 10s, walk back to down dog.
Rag doll pose — 3 sets / 90s
↳ Feet hip-width, deep bend, grab elbows overhead. Full surrender.
Week 6 — The Full Fold (25 min daily)
Integrate everything — reach the floor
Full warm-up flow — 1 round / 5 min
↳ Cat-cow, glute bridges, hip hinges. Get the body ready to move.
Active standing forward fold — 3 sets / 5 pulses + 30s hold
↳ Pulse gently to edge of stretch, then relax and breathe into it.
Toe touch progression — 5 sets / 10s each
↳ Each attempt, breathe out and try to go slightly deeper than before.
Full forward fold hold — 3 sets / 2 min
↳ Flat feet, straight legs or close. Fingertips or palms to floor. You have got this.
