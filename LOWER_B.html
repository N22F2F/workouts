🔴 P10: Vacuum Holds — What are they?
A core drill where you gently draw your abdomen inward
Hold that position while breathing under control
Trains the transverse abdominis — acts like an internal corset
🔵 P10: How to do them
Stand tall, kneel, or lie on your back
Exhale fully
After the exhale, gently pull belly button inward and slightly up
Think zip the waist in, not crunch down
Keep ribs stacked over pelvis
Hold the tension without shrugging or folding over
Breathe lightly if you can, or use short holds after the exhale
This is a drawing-in drill, not bracing as hard as possible
🟢 P10: What you should feel
Deep tension around the waist
A drawing-in feeling through the lower abdomen
Not a hard crunch or braced-out stomach
🧡 P10: How much
Start with 3–5 sets of 15–30 seconds
Quality matters more than forcing longer holds
💜 P10: Why it is in your plan
Helps with waist control
Improves awareness of ribcage / pelvis position
Pairs well with breathing drills, planks, and glute work
Supports the appearance of a tighter midsection
🟠 P10: Common mistakes
Sucking in from the chest only
Holding your breath too aggressively
Rounding forward
Gripping hip flexors or glutes too hard
Confusing it with bracing as hard as possible
🩷 P10: Quick cue
Exhale, ribs down, draw the lower waist inward
Reverse crunch — 2–3 x 10–15
🔴 P9: 90/90 Breathing — What is it?
A breathing drill to get ribs stacked over pelvis
Switches down from a flared-rib posture
Hips at ~90 degrees, knees at ~90 degrees
Usually done lying on back, feet on bench, wall or chair
🔵 P9: How to do it
Lie on your back
Feet flat on wall/bench/chair, hips and knees both around 90°
Lightly tuck pelvis so lower back is gently heavy on floor
Keep ribs down, not flared up
Inhale quietly through your nose
Exhale fully through mouth like fogging a mirror
At end of exhale, feel abs turn on without crunching
Pause briefly
Inhale again without losing rib/pelvis position
Cue: gently pull heels down wall without moving them
🟢 P9: What you should feel
Lower ribs coming down on the exhale
Abs turning on
Less arch in the low back
Sometimes hamstrings lightly if using the wall correctly
🧡 P9: How much
Usually 2 rounds of 3–5 breaths
Quality matters more than rushing
💜 P9: Why it is in your plan
Improves waist control
Helps core positioning for lifts
Can reduce over-arching through the lower back
Pairs well with vacuum holds, planks, and glute training
🟠 P9: Common mistakes
Inhaling into the chest only
Arching the lower back
Forcing a huge pelvic tuck
Pushing with quads instead of lightly using hamstrings
Rushing the breaths
🩷 P9: Quick cue
Exhale fully, ribs down, heels lightly pull down the wall
🔴 P8: RKC Plank — What is it?
A hardstyle plank variation
Looks like a normal forearm plank
Instead of just holding, you create full-body tension
🔵 P8: How to do it
Set up on forearms, elbows under shoulders
Legs straight, feet about hip-width or a bit narrower
Tuck pelvis slightly so ribs stay down
Squeeze glutes hard
Squeeze quads
Pull elbows toward toes without actually moving
Pull toes toward elbows without actually moving
Brace abs as if someone is about to punch you
🟢 P8: What you should feel
Deep abdominal tension
Glutes and quads working
Sometimes lats too
Much more intense than a regular plank
🧡 P8: How long
Usually short holds: 10–30 seconds
Quality matters more than holding forever
💜 P8: Why it is in your plan
Builds waist control and trunk stiffness
Core bracing without side-bending or heavy oblique loading
🟠 P8: Common mistakes
Lower back sagging
Hips too high
Just hanging out instead of creating tension
Holding too long and losing position
🩷 P8: Quick cue
Squeeze everything and drag elbows to toes
